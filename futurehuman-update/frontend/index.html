Questions


What can I use today?

The web app: accounts, screen capture from a shared window, meeting capture with your microphone, search, briefs, drafts and alerts. The macOS/Windows desktop app is in development.

Does a bot join my meetings?

No. In the browser, Futurehuman hears your microphone. Capturing all participants needs the desktop app.

Does it send email for me?

Never. It prepares drafts and you decide whether to send.

How does it decide when to interrupt me?

Each candidate is scored on relevance, urgency and confidence, divided by your cognitive load. It only appears above your threshold, and learns from what you dismiss.
